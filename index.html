NCAA Volleyball Comeback Rates After Losing Set 1

Teams that lose set 1 still win the match in about 21.2% of NCAA Division I matches overall. The rate rises sharply as matches get longer: 33.2% in 4-set matches and 51.5% in 5-set matches.

Comeback rate by match length

3-set matches
0%
4-set matches
33.2%
5-set matches
51.5%

What this means

The longer the match lasts, the more time the trailing team has to recover. In short sweeps, the first-set loss usually becomes a decisive deficit. In 4-set and especially 5-set matches, the comeback path becomes much more realistic.
